About AshaCare

Digital support system for frontline health workers

The Problem

ASHA and Anganwadi workers are expected to manage growing responsibilities — maternal surveillance, child nutrition, immunization tracking, and NCD screening — using paper-based tools with no real-time decision support.

This limits their effectiveness in the communities they serve.

Our Solution

AshaCare is a simple digital platform designed for second-year college project level that helps ASHA and Anganwadi workers:

  • Maintain digital records of beneficiaries
  • Track maternal health and high-risk pregnancies
  • Monitor child growth and nutrition status
  • Manage immunization schedules
  • Conduct and record NCD screenings
  • Get simple alerts and decision support
